Bickers app privacy policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

This policy explains how we handle personal information in the Bickers workforce app and the connected Bickers Booking system. The app is for approved employees and authorised crew. An administrator must approve access before you can sign in.

Who is responsible for your information?

Bickers of Coddenham Ltd, trading as Bickers Action, is the controller of the personal information used for our workforce operations. This app policy supplements our company GDPR policy.

Information the app uses

The information available depends on your role, access permissions and the features you use. It may include:

  • Account and profile information: your name, work email, telephone number, employee or user identifier, role, profile picture, account approval and authentication records.
  • Work information: assigned jobs, schedules, job and site addresses, crew contact details, work notes and information in the Work Diary where your access is authorised.
  • Employment and administration records: timesheets, hours and travel entries, leave requests and absence information, expenses, receipt images and approval status. Absence information may reveal health information if you or an administrator provide it.
  • Reports and documents: vehicle checks, maintenance reports, photographs, attachments, comments and acknowledgements, including working-terms signatures and acceptance records.
  • Notifications and technical information: device notification tokens, platform and app version, notification preferences and read status. Our hosting and authentication services may process connection information such as IP addresses, request times and error or security logs.

We receive information from you, from authorised Bickers administrators and from colleagues recording work or managing requests in the connected system. The app also stores session information, cached work data and pending submissions on your device so it can operate reliably.

Why we use it

We use this information to authenticate users, manage approved access, organise work, provide schedules and contact information, process hours, leave and expenses, maintain vehicles and safety records, record acknowledgements, deliver work notifications and investigate technical or security problems.

Our legal bases depend on the purpose. They include performing our employment or engagement arrangements, meeting legal obligations such as employment, accounting and health-and-safety requirements, and our legitimate interests in running a safe, secure and effective workforce operation. Where we rely on legitimate interests, we consider the impact on the people concerned.

Where necessary, health-related information is handled for employment and social-protection obligations with the applicable legal safeguards. Where we rely on consent for an optional use, you may withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Core account and work information is necessary to provide workforce access and administer your work. Without it, some features or access may be unavailable. Optional device permissions can be declined. We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you solely by automated processing through the app.

Device permissions and your choices

  • Camera, photos and files: used when you choose to add a profile picture, receipt, report photograph or attachment. You can change the app’s permissions in your device settings.
  • Notifications: used for work updates and reminders. You can manage available preferences in the app and disable push notifications in your device settings.
  • Location and contacts: the app displays work locations and the company contact directory; these features do not require continuous GPS tracking or uploading your personal address book.

The app does not include advertising SDKs or cross-app advertising tracking. This privacy page does not load analytics scripts or set optional tracking cookies.

Who can receive information?

Authorised Bickers staff and crew can access information needed for their work, subject to their permissions. For example, administrators process requests and maintain records, and crew may see work assignments and company contact details. Work Diary access is restricted to authorised users.

We use service providers to operate the app and connected system. These include Google Firebase for authentication, database and file storage; Expo for app updates and push-notification delivery; Apple or Google for device push delivery; Render for the mobile API; and Vercel for the Booking website. The Booking website also uses Clerk for website authentication. Providers process the information needed to deliver their services.

Information may also be shared with professional advisers, insurers, authorities or other recipients where necessary for our employment and business responsibilities, a legal requirement or the establishment or defence of legal claims.

Some providers may process information outside the UK. Our company policy requires appropriate safeguards for overseas processing. Depending on the transfer, these may include an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards. Contact us for information about the safeguards relevant to your data.

How long we keep information

We retain information according to its purpose, our company retention schedule and applicable employment, accounting, safety and legal requirements. For example, our company policy generally retains personnel records for six years after employment ends and payroll records for six years from the relevant financial year end. Different periods apply to other operational records.

Access can be disabled when it is no longer authorised. Disabling an account or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete company records that we need to retain. Temporary technical records and device tokens are kept according to their operational purpose. Contact us about a particular record or a deletion request.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing or a portable copy. You can also object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights are subject to legal conditions; for example, we may need to retain records to meet a legal obligation.

To exercise a right, request account closure or ask a privacy question, contact action@bickers.co.uk. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

You can complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. You may contact us first so we can try to resolve your concern, but you do not have to do so.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when the app’s use of personal information changes and revise the date above. Where required, we will also bring significant changes to your attention.
